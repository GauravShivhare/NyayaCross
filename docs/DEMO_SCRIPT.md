### [0:00 - 0:30] Hook & The Pain Point

*Junior litigators spend nights manually scouring through handwritten FIRs, police 161 statements, charge sheets, and medical autopsy reports with highlighters trying to catch if a witness lied.*

*Existing legal tech gives you another generic 'chat with your PDF'. Today, team **chor_chapati_gang** introduces **NyayaCross**—the first AI Trial War Room that turns contradictory evidence into courtroom-ready cross-examination strategies."*

### [0:30 - 1:15] Live Demo: The War Room
*"Here is our benchmark Sessions trial: **State of Maharashtra vs. Vikram Shinde**.*

*Notice right at the top: our contradiction engine calculates an **84% Fatal Damage Score** to the prosecution's ocular evidence.*

*Look at the **Timeline Discrepancy Map**:*
- *In Exhibit P-1 (FIR), complainant Ramesh stated the assault took place at **9:45 PM in pitch dark blackout**.*
- *Yet on oath in court (Exhibit D-1), he testified it was **6:15 PM in broad daylight** to justify seeing the accused's face!*

*Below that, our **Contradiction Matrix** flags 3 fatal contradictions and 1 material omission:*
1. *The 3.5-hour temporal shift.*
2. *The weapon escalation: from a blunt wooden stick told to police, to a 14-inch butcher knife in court.*
3. *And the decisive blow: in court the witness testified the victim was repeatedly stabbed in the chest, yet the **Forensic Autopsy Report (Exhibit P-4)** proves there were **zero stab wounds**, with death caused solely by a blunt fall on a curb."*

### [1:15 - 2:00] The Core Innovation: Question Trees & Impeachment
*"NyayaCross doesn't just stop at finding contradictions—it tells the lawyer **how to destroy the witness on the stand**.*

*When I click on this medical contradiction, it generates a **4-Stage Tactical Question Tree**:*
1. *Stage 1 locks the witness into their claim.*
2. *Stage 2 establishes their prior police statement.*
3. *Stage 3 confronts them with the exhibit.*
4. *Stage 4 produces the exact motion to the judge to mark the contradiction on judicial record!*

*Watch the **Adaptive Branching**:*
- *If the witness concedes ('Yes'), the advocate follow-up traps them with perjury.*
- *If they deny ('No'), it pits them against their own investigating officer.*
- *If they evade ('I don't recall'), it destroys their reliable memory."*
